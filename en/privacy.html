Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

In short: your images never leave your device. All of our free tools process images directly inside your browser. We do not upload your files to any server, we do not store them, and we cannot see them.

1) Image & file processing

Every free 007.gallery tool (background removal, upscaling, portrait blur, convert & compress, text extraction, QR generator, and watermarking) runs entirely inside your browser using JavaScript, WebAssembly and WebGPU.

  • Your images and files are never sent to any server of ours.
  • We keep no copies of your files and have no way to view them.
  • Data is cleared from browser memory as soon as you close the page.

2) AI models

Some tools need to download an AI model once from content delivery networks (such as jsDelivr and Hugging Face). The model is then cached in your browser for later use. During that download, the network provider sees your IP address under its own policies. Your images are never part of this process — only the model is downloaded.

3) Data stored locally

We use your browser's local storage for two purposes only:

  • Language preference (Arabic/English) via localStorage.
  • Model caching via Cache/IndexedDB to avoid re-downloading.

You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings.

4) Metadata in exported files

When you download a PNG image from our tools, we embed metadata inside the file naming the tool used, the source website, and the creation date. This metadata contains no personal information about you, it does not alter your image's pixels, and the rights to your image remain entirely yours.

5) Advertising

We show ads as part of our "pay it forward" model to keep the tools free for everyone. Currently we display ads for our own projects only. In the future we may use third-party providers (such as Google AdSense), which may use cookies to serve relevant ads.

  • Google may use the DoubleClick DART cookie to serve ads based on your visits.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads via Google Ads Settings.
  • Your images and files are never shared with advertisers — they never leave your device in the first place.

6) Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly, anonymised analytics to measure visit counts and improve the service. This data is aggregate and statistical, and is not used to identify you personally.

7) Paid services (coming soon)

The professional services marked "coming soon" will run on cloud servers. When they launch, the image you choose will be uploaded for processing and then deleted once the operation completes. We will update this policy with clear details before enabling them.

8) Children

Our services are suitable for everyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as new services are added. The last-updated date is always shown at the top of this page.

10) Contact

For any privacy enquiry, please contact us via the email address listed on the site.
